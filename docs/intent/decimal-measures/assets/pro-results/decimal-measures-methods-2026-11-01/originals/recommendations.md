# Exact decimal measures: recommendations

**Decision:** build an explicit exact-decimal calculation path over preserved source text, with exact reductions, declared rounding, source-record traces, and saved results. Keep existing approximate measurements on their current path.

**Source baseline:** `byronwall/explorEDA` at `d3920a285f3755a8324998775deb131747e959f5`. This document records proposals for implementation. Numeric-preparation and quantity-calculation summaries are prior proposals, not dependencies already present in that baseline.

The stable `ED-xx` IDs below are shared by [report.md](report.md) and [implementation-plan.md](implementation-plan.md). Proposed public types and signatures are collected in [decimal-contract.ts](decimal-contract.ts); independent expected results are in [decimal-fixtures.json](decimal-fixtures.json).

## Stage interpretation

- **M1 — Standalone ledger loop:** preserve source text; create exact line amounts; compare line-rounded and total-rounded metric results; filter and inspect Rows; save, reopen, and refresh. Exact grouping and once-per entity measures are rejected at every entry point until M2.
- **M2 — Project and grouped ledger loop:** exact query operations, source-aware typed bindings, workers, entity grouping, Pivot, folder files, and complete reopen/refresh.
- **M3 — Saved notebook findings:** exact values, differences, narrow reconciliation, original contributors, and targeted text writeback over the same saved rules.
- **M4 — Optional chart projection:** limited approximate visual coordinates with exact identity and trace. This is not a prerequisite for any earlier stage.

`Explore` identifies implementation questions worth measuring; it does not block M1 or require outside demand. `Defer` leaves a capability outside this contract. `Avoid` rules are correctness and scope boundaries rather than future milestones.

Saved `decimal-state` filters make an exclusion deliberate and visible; their excluded member IDs remain traceable. Undefined invalid results retain their error status and must not silently become missing values. Reconciliation assumes amounts already have the same analyst-declared meaning and currency; the supplied ledger examples use USD and require no currency conversion engine.

The two checks answer different questions. `reconcile(leftFinding,rightFinding)` accepts additive sum findings, including sums rounded after accumulation, from the same finished evaluation, population and grain; complete inputs and a zero delta justify **Totals match**. Means and extrema remain exact findings but are not ledger-total operands. `rowpaircheck(leftField,rightField)` compares existing paired line values; complete pairs and no nonzero row residuals justify **Lines match**. A rounded total does not need an invented per-line allocation, and a zero total residual does not prove that every line matches.

For a sum with an explicit output scale/mode, quantize its wide exact accumulator before checking the 128-digit public coefficient limit. A sum without that policy retains maximum contributor scale and checks its coefficient at that scale. Do not prematurely construct a bounded intermediate decimal that would reject an otherwise valid explicitly rounded result.

## Open implementation questions

Choose exact UI placement within the existing calculation editor, measure actual performance, and settle whether M4's projected charts are worth their adapter cost. These do not change the numerical contract or block the first usable ledger loop. No outside-customer or prior-proposal adoption gate is required.
