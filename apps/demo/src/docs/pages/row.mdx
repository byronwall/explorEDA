## How it reads

<ReadingGuide chart="row">
  <Part at="label">Each row is one category of the chart's field.</Part>
  <Part at="bar">
    Bar length counts its rows, or sums or averages a **Measure** by the
    **Operation** you pick (optionally once per ID). That saves a grouped
    summary that bar charts read too.
  </Part>
  <Part at="filtered">
    While other charts filter, rows rank by the filtered value, drawn over a
    faint all-rows bar (a tick for averages, maxima, and minima). Categories
    with no matching rows leave the chart, and a category you selected here
    stays. Turn on **Hold order** to keep every category in its all-rows order
    instead. When the all-rows bars dwarf the filtered values, set **Scale to**
    filtered rows to fit the axis to the filtered values and hide the all-rows
    bars.
  </Part>
  <Part at="zero">Negative values draw left of the zero line.</Part>
  <Part at="other">
    Set **Top N** to show the same categories, and gather the same members in
    **+N more**, at every size. **Show all** clears it.
  </Part>
  <Part>
    Every category shows. Rows shrink to 12 pixels to fit, then the rest fold
    into a **+N more** row that names them on hover.
  </Part>
  <Part>
    **Use as color field** gives each category a color that other charts
    coloring by that field reuse.
  </Part>
</ReadingGuide>

## Annotations

The **Notes** tab adds lines and bands on the horizontal value axis, and callouts that attach to a row by its category, at the end of its bar. On a chart of a grouped summary, such as `row team group=byTeam`, `rule x=mean`, `median`, `min`, and `max` compute over the groups' values, one per group, including groups past **Top N**.
