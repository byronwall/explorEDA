# Who the unemployed were, 2000 to 2010

**Question.** Did the recession change which industries the unemployed came from?

- **Encoding.** A stack normalized to 100% every month, so band thickness is an industry's share of the unemployed, not its count.
- **Why this form.** A count stack shows the total doubling and buries the mix. Normalizing each month answers the composition question directly; the subtitle and findings carry the size.

## What this design gives up

The total. The demo's streamgraph example shows the same data by count; the notebook in this family shows each industry's rate.
