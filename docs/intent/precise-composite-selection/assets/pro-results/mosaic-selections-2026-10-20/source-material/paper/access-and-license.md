## Original research coverage

The report analyzes the model and its relation to existing source-owned dimensions, activation versus semantic update, query/materialized-view compatibility, sufficient measures, quantization versus exact membership, cardinality, invalidation, evaluation setup, and both appendices. It distinguishes the 2025 paper from the June 2025 benchmark harness and October 2026 implementation. The report and `research-notes/performance-review.md` document unresolved Table 5/harness metadata differences instead of silently reconciling them.

No paper benchmark was executed. Paper timings are published results under the authors' setup, not measurements or performance promises for explorEDA.
