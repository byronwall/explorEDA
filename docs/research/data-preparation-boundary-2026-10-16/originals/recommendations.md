# Recommendations for disposition

**Date:** 2026-10-18. **Repository:** https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA. **Selected baseline:** `b07f3cf3483130f6cc3391bba47a87f616c93321` on `main`.

These are proposed dispositions for Codex to decide during local integration. They do not authorize or claim code changes. There is one primary direction: prove the existing local workflow with documentation and a runnable example. The remaining rows specify its boundaries, one conditional repair, two bounded alternatives, and explicit deferrals. They are not independent feature milestones.

**Disposition meanings:** adopt = accept the research decision or constraint, not claim implementation; investigate = collect the named missing evidence; defer = preserve the existing gate without scheduling work; reject = exclude the specified approach from this mission. **Confidence** separates source certainty from product-fit uncertainty. No user-demand or performance confidence is implied by a high source-confidence rating.

## Integration decision and stop conditions

The research favors **DP-01 with DP-02/03/04/08 as its correctness boundaries**. DP-05 is a conditional repair, DP-06/07 are the only two bounded alternatives, DP-09 preserves deferral, and DP-10 records the scope rejection. Accepting a research recommendation does not mean the proof has passed or a feature has shipped.

Reject documentation sufficiency if the proposed author flow cannot complete and explain the required tasks, or a real author cannot accept the explicit upstream handoff. Record the exact failure before choosing the smallest alternative. A passing 18-row example does not establish demand, maximum supported size, response time, or universal SQL/local consistency.

The source packet is selected text, not a runnable checkout. Referenced helper, read, driver, and chart-consumer internals are outside coverage. No code or repository tests were produced or run in this research. Markdown and archive checks are artifact validation only.
