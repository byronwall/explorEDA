# Dense, in-place chart configuration for explorEDA

**Research status: COMPLETE for the requested read-only research and proposed shaping. No implementation or user-validation claim.** Research date: 2026-10-12. Baseline: [`c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e`](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/commit/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e). Integration branch supplied by the user: `codex/dense-chart-interactions`; its remote existence was not required or tested.

## Decision in one page

Make frequent analytical changes available **where their meaning is already visible**, through a small number of visible encoding controls and shallow object menus that open the same compact editors. Keep ordinary mark click-to-filter immediate. Keep the existing title and axis direct manipulation. Do not make selection an entry step into formatting, require a universal edit mode, or put every setting in a right-click tree.

The favored Alt experiment is **Alt/Option-click to configure the object**, with scatter opening chart Color directly. Trace remains reachable from a clearly separated record/object menu command and the existing header trace browser. This is a proposed reallocation, not an accepted user decision. Its alternative is not just “keep everything”; the strongest simpler design is a visible Color selector alone, with no new mark hit-testing and no modifier remap. Test that comparator seriously. [DCI-03, DCI-04, DCI-15, DCI-16 in recommendations.json](recommendations.json).

The first proof is chart color binding on **scatter points and bubbles**, with categorical/numeric fields, None, an existing filter, and another chart sharing the color scale. Opening/searching must not write state. Explicit acceptance must create or reuse a scale and bind the chart in one notification group. Defer palette editing, arbitrary field drag-and-drop, general chart conversion and broad renderer routing until this path proves useful. Existing seams are [ScatterPlotSettingsPanel](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/components/charts/ScatterPlot/ScatterPlotSettingsPanel.tsx#L21-L36), [FieldSelector](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/components/FieldSelector.tsx#L18-L33), [ComboBox](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/components/ComboBox.tsx), [getOrCreateScaleForField](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/hooks/useColorScales.ts#L141-L156) and [useChartEdit](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/components/charts/InPlace/useChartEdit.ts#L12-L27).

### Why a menu alone is not enough

Right-click → Color by → choose field takes three activations, excluding search typing. So does Settings → Color selector → choose field when Data is already shown. The prospective gain is proximity, target clarity and reduced unrelated navigation—not an automatic click-count victory. A visible Color trigger takes two activations. Alt can also take two, but adds a modifier press, learning and platform risk. The menu earns its place only if it improves the actual sequence over that simpler visible control. These are modeled routes, not measured performance.

## 3. Which changes deserve the shortest routes?

The inventory differentiates changing the *meaning* of a chart from polishing its appearance. Reading a legend frequently does not imply changing its palette frequently. Creating a composition once may involve many structural choices that are rarely revisited during linked exploration. No telemetry or task timing supports a numerical frequency distribution.

| Frequency hypothesis during an applicable exploration | Settings and scenarios | Proposed home |
|---|---|---|
| High | Replace X/Y/category/measure/color fields; select/clear a cohort; change table sort; browse a relevant calendar year. The analyst is asking the next question. | Visible binding/action or short object route; keep direct filtering. |
| Medium, strongly scenario-dependent | Size-by, bins, time grain, Top N, split/stack, facets, scale/range, opacity and point size under overlap, fit method. | Compact target-specific editor with a full-settings escape. |
| Low in repeated analysis, sometimes high in authoring | Palette styling, category hand-colors, title typography, margins, elaborate notes, geometry joins, detailed Composition layouts and calculations. | Existing full/settings editor or explicitly shared workspace editor. |

This ordering is conditional. A scientist tuning overlap may change opacity repeatedly; a report designer may edit category colors heavily once. Numeric encoding and categorical grouping can change analysis, while a fixed palette usually changes presentation. The inventory records both exploration and authoring hypotheses, scenario, evidence confidence and the cost of today’s route rather than collapsing them into one “frequency” score.

Promote a few coherent families after proof: field roles at their labels; operation plus measure at the metric title; bins at the histogram; period at the time summary; supported sort/Top N at categories; split/layout at series; chart-wide facet controls at facet headers. Keep statistical constraints and shared aggregate scope. Averages must not gain additive stacking merely because a compact segmented control fits. [packages/explorEDA/src/components/charts/BarChart/BarChartSettingsPanel.tsx · BarChartSettingsPanel](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/components/charts/BarChart/BarChartSettingsPanel.tsx#L15-L30); [packages/explorEDA/src/components/charts/LineChart/TimeSeriesSettingsPanel.tsx · TimeSeriesSettingsPanel](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/components/charts/LineChart/TimeSeriesSettingsPanel.tsx#L80-L95); [packages/explorEDA/src/components/settings/FacetSettingsTab.tsx · FacetSettingsTab](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/components/settings/FacetSettingsTab.tsx#L29-L44). **DCI-08/09.**

## 4. Competitive evidence: eight products, five families

Selection criteria were relevant, verifiable mechanics rather than prestige or feature count. These are eight products, not eleven counted by platform. Detailed structured target/gesture/control/scope/persistence/undo fields are in `product_mechanics` in [sources.json](sources.json). Every unknown undo granularity is explicitly unknown. All pages were accessed on 2026-10-12; rolling help does not establish a binary build.

### Tableau — BI authoring

Current Desktop/web help connects Marks Color and legend Edit Colors, distinguishing categorical assignments and numerical color controls. Desktop confirmation and web application/reset routes differ. This supports treating the legend as an encoding anchor, not a point-style tool. It does not establish explorEDA’s shared-scale ownership or prove a searchable menu is fastest. [Primary evidence E02](https://help.tableau.com/current/pro/desktop/en-us/viewparts_marks_markproperties_color.htm).

### Power BI — BI authoring with an explicit mode boundary

The July 22, 2026 Desktop preview documentation separates ordinary cross-filter clicks from formatting subselections: double-click or right-click Format enters format mode. It also documents target correction for overlapping elements and limitations for small multiples, mobile layout and full accessibility. Borrow semantic target correction; reject the assumption that modeful on-object formatting is automatically appropriate in a linked-filtering workspace. [Primary evidence E01](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-bi/create-reports/power-bi-on-object-interaction).

### Qlik Sense — configuration during analysis

May 2026 help, updated October 7, 2026, offers an exploration menu from the chart control or right-click without selecting data. Changes are session-local until eligible users Apply; alternative fields must be supplied beforehand. Small-screen access differs, and container restrictions exist. This is the strongest contextual precedent for editing without first selecting a mark, but explorEDA should not inherit a second session-versus-saved configuration model merely to copy the mechanism. [Primary evidence E08](https://help.qlik.com/en-US/sense/May2026/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Sense_Hub/UserInterface/visual-exploration-menu.htm).

### JMP — scientific visual construction

Versioned 19.0 Graph Builder help documents visible variable zones, drag assignment and a non-drag select-variable/choose-zone route. Zone menus can swap or remove variables. Graph-area menu scope differs from general element controls, and a modifier can extend scope. Visible field roles transfer well; scope hidden only in a modifier does not. The version is evidence provenance, not a claim about the newest JMP executable. [E03](https://www.jmp.com/support/help/en/19.0/jmp/about-the-graph-builder-window.shtml); [E04](https://www.jmp.com/support/help/en/19.0/jmp/move-or-remove-variables-in-zones.shtml); [E05](https://www.jmp.com/support/help/en/19.0/jmp/popup-menus-in-graph-builder.shtml).

### Origin/OriginPro — object toolbars for scientific graphs

Official mini-toolbar help documents object-dependent tools, Group/Single choices, Object Manager access and command search. It also acknowledges a faded toolbar can be recalled. This supports evaluating a short, stable object toolbar, especially when scope is visible. It does not demonstrate low accidental-edit rates or good discoverability in dense linked dashboards. [Primary evidence E06](https://docs.originlab.com/origin-help/mini-toolbar/).

### Excel — a well-targeted full editor

Official desktop instructions use an element’s right-click Format action to open a tailored pane; selecting another element retargets it, with ribbon alternatives. The transferable lesson is modest: a focused existing editor can be a strong solution. “In place” need not mean reproducing every field as another floating control. [Primary evidence E07](https://support.microsoft.com/en-au/office/format-elements-of-a-chart).

### Grafana — a useful legend with conflicting click semantics

Official legend help distinguishes color-bar editing and series-label isolation; the latter creates an override, and saving the dashboard persists it. This is a concrete counterexample to copying attractive gestures: series visibility is not a linked row filter, and explorEDA’s category chip includes its swatch. Keep the useful object locality, not the primary-click convention. [Primary evidence E09](https://grafana.com/docs/grafana/latest/visualizations/panels-visualizations/configure-legend/).

### Datawrapper — explicit annotation intent in publication authoring

Vendor scatter guidance offers direct or dropdown label selection, removal by clicking an added label, and an explicit range-highlight action before dragging. Its staged authoring/publication context matters. Borrow explicit one-shot annotation intent; do not borrow point-click editing for ordinary exploration. These Academy articles are older, still served first-party pages, so confidence about current detailed runtime behavior is lower. [E10](https://www.datawrapper.de/academy/customizing-your-scatter-plot-annotate); [E11](https://www.datawrapper.de/academy/how-to-create-a-scatter-plot).

Spotfire axis-selector evidence appeared in search but the target help body was unavailable; RAWGraphs was blocked, and a legacy Datawrapper URL failed. They are recorded separately, not counted as evidenced products or treated as lacking features. Grafana and current-domain Datawrapper material gave more concrete accessible mechanics. No comparison here ranks a product objectively best.

### Transfer across hard problems

Nested targets need semantic ownership, not DOM ancestry alone: a point lies in a series, facet and chart. Dense points need disambiguation only for record-specific tasks; chart color does not need a perfect record hit. Many fields call for search by label and identity, not hundreds of cascading menu rows. Small multiples need an explicit “all panels” owner because the clicked panel does not create independent settings. Editable legends need different verbs for *change field*, *edit shared colors* and *filter category*. These are explorEDA design inferences from the evidence and inspected ownership model, not comparative usability findings. ## 5. Seven genuinely different approaches

### A. Minimal reuse: focus the existing settings editor

A target action opens the existing Data/Axis/Fit section and focuses the relevant control. This is navigation into a familiar editor, not a new editing system. It is lowest risk for complex controls and establishes the baseline comparator. Costs are unrelated controls and possible loss of nearby visual context. Exact section/focus APIs may need a small extension; do not claim every tab is externally targetable today. [ChartSettingsContent](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/components/ChartSettingsContent.tsx#L22-L37); [MainSettingsTab](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/components/settings/MainSettingsTab.tsx#L14-L29); [CompositionQuickMenu](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/components/charts/Composition/CompositionQuickMenu.tsx#L16-L31). **DCI-16.**

### B. Semantic object menus: choose a verb, then a compact editor

The analyst hits an object, selects a scoped action and edits just that concern. Keep at most one menu-to-editor transition; field lists belong in search, not submenus. Strength: rich local vocabulary without permanent chrome. Cost: hidden discovery, target ambiguity and portal/event work. Every important action also needs a visible route. [onPanelContextMenu](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/components/PlotChartPanel.tsx#L605-L620); [useAxisFieldActions](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/components/charts/AxisFieldActions.tsx#L44-L59); [ChartImageMenuSection](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/components/charts/export/ChartImageMenu.tsx#L47-L62). **DCI-02/03.**

### C. Visible encoding labels/chips: edit the chart’s grammar

Color: Region, a field title or a short measure expression becomes a discoverable trigger. No mark targeting or temporary mode is necessary. This is the recommended partner to menus and the strongest simpler standalone alternative. Do not turn every field into a persistent Tableau-like shelf: test one Color control before adding density. ## 6. Gesture and ownership model

The detailed target-by-gesture contract is [gesture-map.csv](gesture-map.csv). The essential priority order is: host read-only guard; active text/editor/nested layer; explicit widget/annotation handle; recognized context invocation; armed optional modifier configuration; renderer-specific ordinary click/drag; unhandled image-menu fallback. This is a proposed precedence model on existing seams, not a request for an enterprise-wide interaction dispatcher. [onPanelContextMenu](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/components/PlotChartPanel.tsx#L605-L620); [useAxisFieldActions](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/components/charts/AxisFieldActions.tsx#L44-L59); [useBrush](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/hooks/useBrush.tsx); [ChartGridLayout](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/components/ChartGridLayout.tsx#L44-L59).

For a primary sequence, record the eligible target and intended gesture at press. A recognized drag remains that drag and suppresses its trailing click. An Alt-armed configuration sequence that moves cancels; this **changes today’s possible Alt-brush path**, with ordinary drag as the replacement. No ordinary click waits for a double-click timer. Keep title second-press editing only on the non-filtering title target. Modifier combinations not explicitly assigned should not acquire surprise editing behavior.

Context invocation must not first select a record or clear filters. Resolve the specific logical object once, preserve existing specialized menus, then append one image-export section. Pointer-origin Shift-right-click bypasses before axis handling. Keyboard Shift+F10 is different: it intentionally requests the application menu and must not be rejected merely because Shift is held. macOS Control-click belongs to context-menu handling; Command-click can retain field inspection. Operating systems can intercept Alt/Option, so there is always a visible alternative. [E14](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Element/contextmenu_event); [E15](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/MouseEvent/altKey); [E20](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/MouseEvent/ctrlKey).

Hit areas should be semantic and stable: axis-band targets behind labels, explicit controls ahead of plot hits, renderer plan coordinates for Canvas marks, and logical IDs for record-specific actions. Do not enlarge every dense point into overlapping invisible buttons. A chart-wide color action can remain available when there is no unambiguous point. A trace action must name the chosen record or ask for a candidate. On source revision, revalidate identity or close; never substitute a new record under stale coordinates. [ScatterPoints](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/components/charts/ScatterPlot/ScatterPlot.tsx); [useAxisEditing](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/components/charts/InPlace/useAxisEditing.tsx#L118-L133). **DCI-02/03.**

Dismissal is also a gesture. Clicking outside an open menu should dismiss it without that same sequence filtering the plot; the next independent click uses the immediate filter behavior. This does not justify delaying clicks when no editor is open. Escape closes/cancels the innermost layer and is consumed, rather than also clearing filters or closing details. Return focus to the surviving invoker, then chart header if needed—not the page body. [onPanelContextMenu](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/components/PlotChartPanel.tsx#L605-L620); [ComboBox](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/components/ComboBox.tsx); [useBrush](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/hooks/useBrush.tsx). **DCI-11.**

## 7. Editing semantics and responsive behavior

**Field binding is discrete, not hover-previewed.** Opening/searching/arrow navigation is read-only. Accept a compatible field to commit and close. Preserve current filters and layout. Use the current stable field ID rather than the display alias. Before acceptance, Escape changes nothing. Reopening reads fresh store state, focuses search and starts with an empty query; recent-command memory is not first-slice scope. [FieldSelector](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/components/FieldSelector.tsx#L18-L33); [ComboBox](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/components/ComboBox.tsx); [getOrCreateScaleForField](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/hooks/useColorScales.ts#L141-L156). **DCI-03/05/10.**

**Continuous chart-only properties can preview.** Opacity, ranges and title text apply valid drafts using `useChartEdit`; Enter/Done, appropriate blur or drag release commits the session, and Escape restores only the owned keys. Invalid numeric drafts remain visible with an error and never apply. Finish a continuous edit before another chart mutation can be included in the global notification hold. Unmount behavior must be deliberate, because the existing hook finishes the edit. Do not replace the workspace snapshot as “cancel.” [useChartEdit](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/components/charts/InPlace/useChartEdit.ts#L12-L27); [holdStateChanges](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/providers/DataLayerProvider.tsx#L325-L340); [packages/explorEDA/src/components/charts/InPlace/AxisLimitFields.tsx · AxisLimitFields](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/components/charts/InPlace/AxisLimitFields.tsx#L41-L56). **DCI-05.**

## 8. First proof, later sequence and rejection

Build four local variants only as needed: **A** existing settings route; **B** visible Color-only selector; **C** B plus semantic menu; **D** C with experimental Alt configuration and trace replacement. Compare C against B before calling the menu worthwhile, and D against C before changing the modifier convention. Menu scope, no-legend entry, field search and shared-state safety matter more than adding five other settings. [proof-plan.md](proof-plan.md).

First establish the ordinary unsized-point selection discrepancy with real events, while keeping bubbles/category filtering as positive controls. The proof must show zero unintended filter writes on opening, searching, cancelling and accepting Color; one checkpoint for a new scale plus binding; no peer palette/binding edits; complete keyboard/touch-visible routes; and correct details/focus/export behavior. These are **proposed rejection gates**, not observed results. A compact menu that is slower or harder to discover than B, without an offsetting context benefit, should lose.

After a successful proof, promote bindings and the strongest analytical controls in small coherent groups. Keep rare or structurally dangerous settings in existing editors. Expand trace migration only with replacement access and updated conventions across eligible targets; do not ship Alt meanings that silently differ between ordinary point, title and legend. Shared palette scope/forking, facet rules, annotation commands and specialty controls each need their own ownership proof. No general command registry, renderer rewrite, framework conversion, saved-schema migration or new dependency is justified by this research. [BaseChartSettings](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/types/ChartTypes.ts#L98-L113); [updateChart](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/providers/DataLayerProvider.tsx#L1125-L1140); [CompositionQuickMenu](https://github.com/byronwall/explorEDA/blob/c534378c052c9fbb2cb076d1df0d21eb0fa0965e/packages/explorEDA/src/components/charts/Composition/CompositionQuickMenu.tsx#L16-L31). **DCI-04/07/08/09/13/14/15.**

## 9. Decisions and remaining uncertainty

Two unresolved product judgments should govern the first proof: **Does Alt configuration provide enough repeated-task value to replace trace’s current shortcut?** And **does a persistently readable Color trigger justify its space, or should it be revealed on focus/hover while remaining visible on touch?** Test visibility before relying on undocumented right-click discovery.

Implementation uncertainties are separate: real unsized-point behavior, platform modifier conflicts, dialog portal/focus details, duplicate field aliases, 500-field cost, and multi-write failure rollback. Frequency estimates remain hypotheses until observed analyst tasks or telemetry replace them. Older vendor pages and rolling product versions limit claims about exact current runtime. Source evidence supports the chosen direction and bounded proof, not a claim that the design is already validated.
