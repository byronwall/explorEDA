Heatmaps can show long histories: separate row and column limits (up to 200) and order, a source-order option, and Column dates that bin a date field into years, months, weeks, or days with every period shown and date-range cell selection. Color ends can be fixed, with an optional square-root scale, so a value keeps its color through filters. Numeric row and column values print plainly unless their field has a display format.
