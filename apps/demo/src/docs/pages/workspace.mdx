## Toolbar

- **Add chart** opens a dialog to pick a chart type and its fields.
- **Fields** (<kbd>F</kbd>) lists every field with search, quick stats, and chart actions. <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>F</kbd> shows every field's distribution in a full view. See <DocsLink topic="fields">Fields</DocsLink>.
- **Rows** (<kbd>R</kbd>) peeks at the rows that pass every chart filter.
- **Calculations**, **Theme**, **Colors**, **Grid**, and **Chart spec** open workspace settings: calculated fields, chart type and surfaces, color scales, grid columns and spacing, and what each chart saves.
- **Workspace actions** holds save, open, copy data, and keyboard shortcuts (<kbd>?</kbd>).
- A host app can add its own panels beside Fields and Rows, such as the demo's **Dashboard text** and **History**. One panel is open at a time.

## Chart header

Each chart's header holds its title and its tools: view data (<kbd>V</kbd>), settings (<kbd>S</kbd>), duplicate (<kbd>D</kbd>), delete (<kbd>X</kbd>), trace, and details. Hover a chart, or move to it with <kbd>Tab</kbd>, to use the letter keys.

**Details** opens the chart larger, with a hint for its gestures and tabs for its settings and the rows behind it.

When a title doesn't fit, the header says so. Widen or heighten the chart, or shorten the title. A good title states the question the chart answers, such as "How long does delivery take?".

## Filter bar

The bar under the charts shows how many rows pass every chart filter, followed by a chip for each active filter. Click a chip to jump to the chart that owns it, or remove it from the chip. **Clear all filters** removes chart filters, table searches, and Rows filters at once.

## Layout

Charts sit on a grid. Drag a chart by its title to move it, and drag an edge to resize it. **Grid** sets the column count, row height, and spacing. Below 960 pixels wide, the grid flows charts two to a row, and a chart wider than half the grid takes a whole row.

explorEDA supports desktop viewports of 1024 CSS pixels or more.

## Names for assistive technology

Marks, controls, and charts have accessible names that state what they show, such as "Home: 168 rows" for a bar. The steps on this site use those names to check each example.
