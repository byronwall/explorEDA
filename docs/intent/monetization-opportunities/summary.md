# The research in a few minutes

**Current direction:** Byron prefers recurring SaaS, especially Shopify and DevOps. Start with the [new ten-idea shortlist](recurring-saas-shortlist.md). The summary below preserves the meaning of Pro's original report; its service ranking is no longer the suggested reading order.

**The strongest recurring theme is explaining costly exceptions through their source records.** Examples include a margin problem, repeated CI runs, or scrap costs. Pro sees explorEDA as the investigation workspace inside a more specific customer workflow.

The report contains **75 ideas across 12 families**: 29 to validate, 32 with unresolved dependencies, and 14 rejected as first products. None has proven paid demand. All ideas remain in the [full report](sources/exploreda-monetization-research/exploreda-monetization-opportunities.md) and [catalogue](sources/exploreda-monetization-research/opportunity-catalog.csv).

## The main qualification

**Pro's ranking favors a quick first sale, often through a service. It does not identify a proven SaaS winner.** The prompt supplied a two-to-eight-week pilot assumption and gave weight to delivery speed. Byron has not adopted that deadline or business model.

This explains why a migration review leads the list. The new SaaS shortlist compares repeat demand, setup effort, support, and differentiation separately. Small differences in Pro's scores should not decide that choice.

## What remains for the product to do

Pro's leading proposals mostly reuse existing component features. The surrounding app must establish trustworthy inputs, define the business metric, save the investigation, and help the customer act. A domain formula or connector is not automatically a component defect.

The [shared roadmap](sources/exploreda-monetization-research/exploreda-monetization-opportunities.md#8-consolidated-component-and-application-roadmap) points to three reusable pieces: import checks, saved investigation snapshots, and an explicit action list. It treats a public selected-row API as a possible later extension, not a universal prerequisite.

## How much to trust it

Treat it as a researched set of hypotheses. Pro inspected source and test files but ran no repository tests, live demo, or customer pilot. It reported a newer `main` revision than the intake checkout. Its 250 source records are preserved; their claims were not independently re-researched during intake.

The business preference is now clear. Compare one commerce subscription with one developer-tool subscription using the SaaS shortlist. Check whether new data creates another useful customer action without another manually written report.
