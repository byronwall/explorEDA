`ExplorEdaNotebook` shows a notebook's prose and charts in one reading column, and project folders list `.eda.md` notebooks beside analyses. A chart's clicks and brushes stay with that chart there (`localSelections` on `DataLayerProvider`), and a block with broken text keeps its last results until it is fixed. Line charts now draw an ISO date x field with calendar ticks.
