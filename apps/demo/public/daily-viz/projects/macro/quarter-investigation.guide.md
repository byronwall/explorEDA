# Investigate U.S. quarters by growth, jobs, and prices

## Try this

1. Brush negative GDP growth and inspect the remaining quarters in the record table.
2. Select a decade and verify the intersection, including the first-quarter missing-change behavior.
3. Clear all filters before comparing the fixed narrative extrema with live charts.

## Limits

This is an old source vintage, not current revised GDP. Annualizing a quarterly change is a mathematical convention, not the year-on-year rate. The first quarter has no preceding observation in this file.
