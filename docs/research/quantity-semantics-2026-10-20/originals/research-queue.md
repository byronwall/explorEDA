## 6. Conditions that close or reopen this queue

**Keep feature adoption NO-GO** if no repeated task rejects current controls, quantity/reference meaning is unknown, incompatible declarations remain unexplained, stored numbers would be silently reinterpreted, or the smallest proposed solution needs a broad engine.

**Close the quantity follow-up** when the author succeeds with current labels/descriptions/formulas or upstream preparation and has no recurring unresolved task. A complete defer decision is useful; do not keep a generic “more quantity research” item alive.

**Reopen narrowly** for one of three things:

1. A material scientific declaration is missing and needed to answer the actual task.
2. A reproducible existing-controls failure is observed after a fair lesson.
3. A repeated downstream task rejects the existing export or interpretation boundary.

Human choices remain open: which task to test, its reference population and weights, whether standalone CSV must carry interpretation, whether a lesson is worth maintaining, and what burden would justify a structured property. This research does not choose those policies by convenience. No clarification blocks completion of the research package.
