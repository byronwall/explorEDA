## Integration judgments

The category counts remain useful. The integration changes their presentation and scope:

- “Small extension” means bounded ownership, not a promise of low effort. Exact sparklines, focus populations, and numeric summaries need substantial work.
- An available analytical alternative does not reproduce every arrangement or exported image. Keep those losses explicit.
- No general advanced-settings page is proposed. Place operations in familiar tabs and reveal only relevant details.
- Existing facet order, numeric colors, chart-local filters, log axes, and keyed Notes reduce several proposed gaps.
- Apply current repository rules for tooltips and keyboard focus. The raw report's uncertainty does not override those instructions.
- Use the actual field **Yield gap**, not the report's shortened **Gap**, when authoring that counterpart.

No material research gap requires a Pro follow-up. The remaining questions concern future product proof or implementation detail.
The original artifacts remain unchanged. The [source checks](source-checks.md) record independent evidence and limits.
