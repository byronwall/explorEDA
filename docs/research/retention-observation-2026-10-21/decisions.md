# Six research dispositions

These are review decisions about proposals. They do not approve a new application feature.

| ID   | Disposition | Reason and destination                                                                                                                                                          |
| ---- | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| RO01 | Adopt       | Keep the event-snapshot contract. Record its limits in the domain intent and research summary. Reject inferred continuous collection.                                           |
| RO02 | Investigate | Keep local trust first. Then compare the eight-row fixture with planner/provider/browser behavior. No new proof passed here.                                                    |
| RO03 | Defer       | Existing trace and docs may suffice. Pair one lesson with a runnable example only after a consequential misunderstanding.                                                       |
| RO04 | Investigate | Compare upstream authored coverage with the smallest relevant alternative for a named task. Count lost lineage and author effort.                                               |
| RO05 | Defer       | A scalar endpoint has no established demand or scope. Require authoritative population, interval, event types and boundary convention. Reject it for gaps or unequal coverage.  |
| RO06 | Investigate | Keep reached-cohort All now. Evaluate disclosure versus partial exclusion separately after a summary interpretation failure. Do not equate nonpartial with complete collection. |

Reject automatic completeness inference, fabricated zero events, implicit denominator changes, a coverage engine and a survival model for this mission.
One clear task failure can justify a bounded comparison. Do not require all alternatives to fail in a fixed sequence.
The day-rounding source/prose discrepancy is a specific contract check, independent of demand for metadata.
