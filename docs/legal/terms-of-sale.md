These terms cover buying explorEDA licenses at exploreda.dev. Use of the
software is covered by the [commercial license agreement](commercial-license.md).

## Who you buy from

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com
is the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Paddle provides all customer
service inquiries and handles returns. Paddle's
[buyer terms](https://www.paddle.com/legal/invoiced-consumer-terms) apply to
the payment.

{{SELLER_ADDRESS}}.

## What you buy

- **Commercial**: a license for a number of developers, for 12 months of
  updates, renewing yearly. Versions released during those 12 months stay
  licensed for good.
- **Redistribution add-on**: one per product that customers install
  themselves or that exposes explorEDA to other developers.
- **Enterprise**: a quoted order for 10 or more developers, paid by invoice.

Prices are listed at https://exploreda.dev/pricing in US dollars. Paddle adds
sales tax or VAT where it applies and may show prices in your currency.

## Delivery

Your license key and a link to your license certificate are emailed to the
purchaser as soon as payment clears, usually within a minute. Invoiced orders
are delivered when the invoice is paid. If the email doesn't arrive, write to
support@exploreda.dev with your order number.

## Renewal and cancellation

Licenses renew automatically each year at the price shown on your renewal
reminder, sent 30 days before. Cancel any time before the renewal date from
the billing link in your receipt or by writing to support@exploreda.dev. When
you cancel, you keep using the versions you already have.

## Adding seats

Add developers at any time from your license page. You pay a prorated amount
for the rest of the current year and receive a new key.

## Purchase orders and invoices

For a quote, an invoice, or payment by bank transfer, use the quote form at
https://exploreda.dev/pricing or write to sales@exploreda.dev. We can sign
your vendor forms. Terms printed on a purchase order do not change these
terms.
