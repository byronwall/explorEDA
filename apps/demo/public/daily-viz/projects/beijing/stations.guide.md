# Twelve stations, one city's air

## Try this

1. Select **Dingling**, a station in the northern hills, then **Dongsi** in the city center. The calendars look alike: episodes cover the whole city. Dongsi's mean runs about a third higher.
2. Brush the high end of the wind axis. Almost every windy day is a clean day, at every station.
3. Select **Winter** in the box plot and look at the calendar: winter is clean most days and terrible on a few.

## Limits

Station means compare locations over the same days, but stations miss different days during outages.
