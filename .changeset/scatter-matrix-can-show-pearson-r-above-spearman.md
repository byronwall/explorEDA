Scatter Matrix can show Pearson r above Spearman ρ in each correlation cell (Correlation: Both). A new Pairs action in the chart header lists every pair of number and date fields with r, ρ, and rows, sorted by field order, |ρ|, |r|, or |ρ − r|, with search and a link to each pair's trace.
