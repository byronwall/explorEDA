M1 · the chart

Which fields are missing together?

Each row is one exact pattern: the filled dots are missing, every other chosen field is present. Click rows to select a union of patterns. The selection filters the dashboard like any other chart.

Order data gaps
Opening state: the two selected patterns are orders with no tracking number and no delivery date. The peer chart shows they are almost all Store orders (picked up in person), so this gap is expected, not a data bug. Try adding customer_phone or review_text.
Orders by channel
Peer chart. Blue is the part that matches the selected patterns.

3Union of exact patterns. "tracking + delivered" does not include rows that are also missing ship date. That is a separate row.
