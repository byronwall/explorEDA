# Winter's air has the longest tail

**Question.** Is winter worse every day, or worse on some days?

- **Encoding.** One density ridge per month on a shared PM2.5 axis and a shared peak height, so a tall, narrow ridge means most days were alike and a low, wide one means they were not. A guide marks China's 24-hour limit of 75 µg/m³.
- **Why this form.** Twelve box plots would show the medians and hide the shape. Ridges show December's long right tail and August's tight bump together.

## What this design gives up

Station identity and the order of days within a month. The calendar in the series shows when the bad days fell.
