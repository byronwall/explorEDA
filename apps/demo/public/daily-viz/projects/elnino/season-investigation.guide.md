# Investigate warm months against their own season

## Try this

1. Select a decade, then brush positive anomalies in the histogram; the line should narrow to the same months.
2. Alt-click a point and subtract its climatology from its Celsius temperature.
3. Clear the decade selection without clearing the anomaly brush and compare the broader population.

## Limits

These are monthly temperatures for Niño 1+2, not a global ocean average or a Niño 3.4 event classification. The baseline is the 1981–2010 calendar-month mean; no modern data revision is substituted.
