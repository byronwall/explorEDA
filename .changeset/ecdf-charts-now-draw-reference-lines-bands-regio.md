ECDF charts now draw reference lines, bands, regions, and callouts, on values along x and shares along y. Heatmaps and calendars take callouts on a cell or day (`category=Tue,9`, `on=date:2016-12-16`), and `rule x=mean`, `median`, `min`, and `max` now draw on histograms and on grouped row charts.
