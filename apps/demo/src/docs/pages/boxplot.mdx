## How it reads

<ReadingGuide chart="boxplot">
  <Part at="box">Each box spans the middle half of a group's values.</Part>
  <Part at="median">The line across the box is the median.</Part>
  <Part at="whisker">
    **Whisker type** sets how far whiskers reach: 1.5 times the interquartile
    range, the minimum and maximum, or standard deviations.
  </Part>
  <Part at="outlier">
    **Show Outliers** draws values beyond the whiskers as separate points.
  </Part>
  <Part at="violin">
    **Display** can add a violin: a density shape around each box, smoothed
    automatically from the group's spread and row count, or by hand.
  </Part>
  <Part at="observations">
    **Observations** shows individual values over each box, up to the first 300
    valid rows per group.
  </Part>
</ReadingGuide>

## Annotations

The **Notes** tab adds lines and bands on the value axis, such as a specification limit or a normal range.
