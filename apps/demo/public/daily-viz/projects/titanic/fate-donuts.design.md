# Three classes, three different odds

**Question.** How did a passenger's class change their odds?

- **Encoding.** One ring per class; arc length is the share of that class's passengers who survived or died. Colors stay fixed across rings.
- **Why this form.** Three rings answer "what share of this class survived" at a glance, which is the question here. Comparing counts across classes would need bars; the class sizes differ by more than two to one.

## What this design gives up

Counts and sex. Third class had more than twice as many passengers as first; the notebook in this family shows sex and class together.
