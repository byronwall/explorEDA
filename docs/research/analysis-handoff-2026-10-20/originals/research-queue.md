## Separate result-reuse demand gate

AH05 retains the existing intent's boundary. The following evidence must identify a real downstream job before result-table work moves into the queue:

- **Repeated use or a blocked dependent view:** What exact output is exported and imported again, or what second view cannot use existing source operations?
- **Result grain:** Is one row an observation, experiment mean, group, model sample or bin? Generated curve points must not be treated as independent observations.
- **Contributors and reference:** Which inputs and exclusions produce each row, and what population remains necessary to interpret it?
- **Filter and refresh behavior:** Which upstream selections affect it, when is it recomputed, and what should a downstream selection mean?
- **Consumer and alternatives:** Why do current calculations/query steps or explicit preparation not meet the task at acceptable effort?

Only then compare one inspectable result table feeding one current view. A schema, generic result catalogue, automatic lineage graph, compatibility layer or larger bundle is not the first experiment. The existence of project queries, an export request, a successful guide, a companion trial or an external product feature does not satisfy this gate. [G3]
