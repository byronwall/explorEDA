From editorial chart styling. Bar, row, scatter, line, box, histogram, and ECDF take the full theme typography. Every other family takes only the themed header: data table, pivot, summary, metric card, markdown, map, calendar, heatmap, 3D scatter, Sankey, parallel coordinates, and color legend. Give each its themed axis or text sizes and check it under Compact, Newsprint, and Report in light and dark.
