## Evidence interpretation

**Observed** means supported by a readable implementation, test definition, package contract, or intent statement at the baseline. Reading a test shows what the test expresses; it does not establish that the test passed in this environment. **Inference** means a risk or opportunity derived from that source. **Proposed** means an alternative, fixture, experiment, user study, or implementation direction that has not been executed here. Confidence is confidence in the research opportunity, with gaps identified in each brief.

Current source and tests take precedence over stale inventory or planning language. The complete intent reconciliation below records that distinction. ### Exact content-reading ledger

“Full” records a complete text read. “Targeted” records relevant source functions, test cases, document sections, or lifecycle/decision fields, including reference verification. Multiple readers are deduplicated; a full read takes precedence over a targeted one. Paths link to the verified commit. Assets, omitted datasets, generated outputs, dependencies, and unavailable historical archives are not presented as reviewed material.
