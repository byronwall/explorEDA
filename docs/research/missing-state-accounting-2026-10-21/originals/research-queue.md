## Source-predicted R051 referral from R026

At the pinned baseline, `packages/explorEDA/src/hooks/useFilteredFieldProfiles.ts` recognizes both charts and workspace filters when selecting globally filtered rows. The supplementary pinned `packages/explorEDA/src/hooks/useFieldDistribution.ts`, `useFieldDistributions`, checks only `charts.length > 0` before requesting filtered IDs. With zero charts and an active workspace filter, this predicts possible all-row distributions beside filtered profiles. Execute that exact condition before proposing any change. Keep it in R051 because it concerns population ownership rather than the meaning of null or invalid values.

The ordinary R026 ledger should keep at least one chart present and filter on `cohort`, not the converted field `x`. `DataLayerProvider.updateFieldSettings` intentionally clears filters on x when its type, date preset, or null tokens change; that lifecycle must not masquerade as a value-accounting mismatch.
