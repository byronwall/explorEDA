Compositions can draw bands: a filled area between two fields along x in the order of a field, such as a forecast's 10th to 90th percentile, layered widest first and broken at missing bounds. A numeric scale spans every field drawn on it, so bands stay in the frame. Guides can run horizontally at a numeric y value, and either direction can shade the frame on one side, such as a projection period.
