# Which airline, from which airport, ran late?

## Try this

1. Select **ExpressJet Airlines Inc.** in the airline list. Its flights come almost entirely from Newark, and the histogram's tail grows: its mean delay is more than twice the month's.
2. Clear it and select **Delta Air Lines Inc.** Its tail is short, and the median drops below zero.
3. In the flow chart, select **LGA** to see which airlines carry LaGuardia's traffic, then compare the pivot's medians.

## Limits

An airline's delay depends on where and when it flies. ExpressJet runs short regional hops out of Newark at busy hours; part of its number belongs to the airport and the schedule, not the airline.
