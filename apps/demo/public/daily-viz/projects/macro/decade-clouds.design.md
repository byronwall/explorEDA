# Decade clouds complicate a pooled economic relationship — design and analytical contract

**Question.** Does the pooled historical association resemble the association within the 1970s?

**Encoding.** Individual quarterly observations are positioned by unemployment and inflation and colored by decade.
**Ordering.** No connecting path: file order is not a trajectory through this two-variable space.
**Baseline.** Inflation includes a zero reference in the domain; source initial placeholder remains identifiable in the table.
**Scale.** Shared linear percentage scales for every decade.

## Fixed-snapshot findings

The pooled inflation–unemployment correlation, excluding the initial placeholder quarter, was 0.065. Eligible rows: 202; full expression, denominator, and contributing IDs are in the [evidence](../../evidence/findings/cp-macro-decade-clouds.json).

Within the supplied 1970s quarters, the correlation was -0.183. Eligible rows: 40; full expression, denominator, and contributing IDs are in the [evidence](../../evidence/findings/cp-macro-decade-clouds.json).

## What this design gives up

The graphic discards temporal adjacency. Pooled or grouped proximity is not a causal policy relationship.

Time ordering, policy regimes, revisions, and omitted factors limit a pooled scatter. The source’s initial inflation placeholder is excluded from the pooled calculation. Correlation is not a policy trade-off or causal estimate.

## Interactions and tracing

In viewing mode, selecting a repeated unit is intended to filter that repeat across a workspace. Alt-click traces contributing marks. These behaviors come from the inspected baseline contract; this artifact has not been browser-tested. Static findings above describe the unfiltered snapshot.
