Compositions can now place points and paths at numeric x and y. Add an X–Y unit, or change a mark's type to Points or Path. Numeric scales map a field along a frame with a shared or per-unit extent, an optional zero baseline, tidy ends, and fixed limits that clip marks to the frame. A path connects a repeat's rows in the order of a field such as year and breaks at a missing value. Annotations can follow the point at a chosen value, guides can sit at a numeric x, and the trace shows a path's order, runs, and skipped rows.
