Row and bar charts can show a rate (one sum divided by another, per group), each bar's share of the total, and a number format (percent, or plain with decimals). Rates and averages show n in the readout and fade groups with fewer than 10 rows. Scatter plots can label points by a field, and dashboard text warns when x.min or x.max is set on a date axis.
