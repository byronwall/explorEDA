# Privacy policy

{{SELLER_LEGAL_NAME}} ("we") sells explorEDA licenses. This policy explains
what we collect and why.

## The explorEDA package collects nothing

The `exploreda` package makes no network requests. It sends no telemetry,
usage data, or license checks. License keys are checked in your browser.

## What the website and license service keep

- **Purchases**: Paddle, our merchant of record, collects payment and billing
  details under [its own privacy notice](https://www.paddle.com/legal/privacy).
  We receive your name, email, company, country, tax ID if given, and order
  details. We never see card numbers.
- **Licenses**: the licensee name, email, seat count, plan, dates, and the
  keys we issued.
- **Trials, community keys, and quote requests**: the name, email, company,
  and message you submit.
- **Email delivery**: our email provider ({{EMAIL_PROVIDER}}) processes
  addresses to deliver license emails.
- **Website**: exploreda.dev is a static site. It sets no tracking cookies.
  Our host may keep standard server logs.

## Why

To deliver and support your license, send renewal reminders and receipts,
answer quote requests, prevent abuse of trials, and meet tax and accounting
duties. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

## How long

License and order records: for the life of the license plus 7 years for tax
records. Trial and quote records: 2 years. Ask us to delete anything we are
not required to keep.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export, or delete your data, or object to its
use, by writing to privacy@exploreda.dev. If you are in the EU or UK, you can
also complain to your data protection authority.

## Processors

Paddle (payments), Cloudflare (license service hosting), {{EMAIL_PROVIDER}}
(email). Data may be processed in the United States.

## Changes

We'll post changes here and update the version above.
