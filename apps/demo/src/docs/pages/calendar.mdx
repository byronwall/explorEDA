## When to use it

Use a calendar heatmap to see a daily count or measure across a year, laid out as weeks, so weekday rhythms and seasonal runs stand out.

## How it reads

- Each cell is one day of the date field, shaded by its count of rows or by a summary of a measure.
- Each column is a week, starting on Sunday or Monday.
- The chart shows one year: the year you choose, or the latest year with dates.
