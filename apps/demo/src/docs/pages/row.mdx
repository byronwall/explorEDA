## How it reads

<ReadingGuide chart="row">
  <Part at="label">Each row is one category of the chart's field.</Part>
  <Part at="bar">
    Bar length counts its rows, or sums or averages a **Measure** by the
    **Operation** you pick (optionally once per ID). That saves a grouped
    summary that bar charts read too.
  </Part>
  <Part at="filtered">
    While other charts filter, the filtered value draws over a faint all-rows
    bar (a tick for averages, maxima, and minima), and rows keep their all-rows
    order.
  </Part>
  <Part at="zero">Negative values draw left of the zero line.</Part>
  <Part at="other">
    Set **Top N** to keep the same categories, and the same members in
    **Other**, at every size.
  </Part>
  <Part>
    Row height stays between the minimum and maximum you set; when rows would be
    shorter, fewer categories show.
  </Part>
  <Part>
    **Use as color field** gives each category a color that other charts
    coloring by that field reuse.
  </Part>
</ReadingGuide>

## Annotations

The **Notes** tab adds lines and bands on the horizontal value axis, and callouts that attach to a row by its category, at the end of its bar. On a chart of a grouped summary, such as `row team group=byTeam`, `rule x=mean`, `median`, `min`, and `max` compute over the groups' values, one per group, including groups past **Top N**.
