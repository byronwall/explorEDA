## Why this mission

Unit labels, descriptions, numeric formats and explicit calculations already exist. The new question concerns their boundary. A concentration residual keeps its unit while changing its reference. An annual volume differs from an instantaneous flow rate. Celsius absolute values and differences need distinct treatment. Current corpus text already explains some distinctions. This is source evidence for a comparison, not a verified defect or observed demand.

Compare a short lesson and explicit preparation with one bounded metadata proposal. Reject a unit engine without a repeated task. Do not reopen rates or time-series axes. PR454 owns axes.
