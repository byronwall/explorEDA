## Open chart settings

Use the settings button in a chart's header, or hover a chart and press <kbd>S</kbd>. The panel opens beside the chart and leaves it in view, so you see each change as you make it.

## Try it

Switch the histogram to category counts. The chart redraws at once, with one bar for each delivery time and one for the orders that have none.

## Tabs

The tabs depend on the chart type:

- **Data** chooses the fields and what each mark counts or measures.
- **Fit** adds regression fits, on scatter plots.
- **Facets** splits a chart into small multiples by one or two fields.
- **Axes** sets each axis's scale, tick density, grid, and range.
- **Filters** edits the chart's selection by hand.
- **Labels** sets the title, subtitle, and source note, their type size and weight, and the axis titles.
- **Spacing** sets the margins around the plot.

## Changes and Reset

Every valid edit applies right away. **Reset** undoes every change made since the panel opened, in one step. A range whose minimum passes its maximum is not applied until you fix it.
