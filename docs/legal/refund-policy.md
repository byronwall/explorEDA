# Refund policy

If explorEDA doesn't work for you, ask for a full refund within 30 days of
purchase or renewal. No reason is needed. Write to support@exploreda.dev or
reply to your receipt with your order number.

Refunds are processed by Paddle, our merchant of record, to the original
payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days.

After a refund, the license and its key stop being valid for commercial use.
Free use under the explorEDA License remains available if you qualify.

Seats added mid-term can be refunded within 30 days of adding them.

After 30 days, we refund only where the law requires it, or when we can't
fix a defect that stops the software working as documented.
