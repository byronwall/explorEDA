## How it reads

- The table shows the rows that pass every other chart's filters.
- **Distributions in headers** draws each field's distribution under its column name. Click or drag a distribution to filter that column.
- Search and sorting change what this table shows. Search does not filter other charts.

## Sorting, filtering, and formatting

- Click a column heading to sort by it, and again to reverse the order.
- Right-click a heading or cell to filter, sort, move, hide, or copy.
- Drag a column by its name to reorder it, or drag it away from the header to hide it.
- Columns fit their names and values.
- Values follow each field's format settings, such as currency, units, and precision.
- The table draws only the rows in view, so long tables scroll quickly.
