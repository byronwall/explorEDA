## How it reads

<ReadingGuide chart="hierarchy">
  <Part at="level">
    Levels are category fields from outermost in, one to six. Each row is one
    path through them.
  </Part>
  <Part at="size">
    Node size is a count of rows, or a sum of a measure. Negative and missing
    measure values are left out.
  </Part>
  <Part at="color">
    Nodes are colored by their first-level branch, by level, or in one color.
  </Part>
  <Part>
    **Arrangement** draws the same hierarchy as a treemap, an icicle, or a
    sunburst.
  </Part>
  <Part>
    Zoom into a branch to see its parts; the zoom saves with the chart.
  </Part>
</ReadingGuide>

## Choose an arrangement

- **Treemap** fits the most leaves in a box. Use it for two or three levels where leaf sizes matter most.
- **Icicle** gives each level its own column, so labels stay readable at every depth and a parent sits beside its children. Use it for deep, ordered paths such as call stacks or folders. A profiler's flame graph is an icicle. See [where one request spends its time](/examples/request-profile).
- **Sunburst** draws the icicle in rings. Outer rings get more room than inner ones, so compare sizes within a ring, not across rings.
