# Product direction from the annotated answers

End users drive the product. Engineering and scientific experiment analysis is a central direction. Real datasets are not yet established; Byron reports another worktree investigating them.

The first pass covers editorial styling, editing in place, the scatter matrix, and table usability. This is a set of four priorities, not a ranked order among them. Time is important and belongs in a second pass. Named comparisons have low current interest.

The live workspace should feel editorial. Named references are the Financial Times, The Economist, The Washington Post, The New York Times, and annual or investor-relations reports. Charts follow global themes and colors automatically unless deliberately overridden. Overrides need a findable view.

Text and axes lead direct editing. Valid edits update immediately and use existing undo. The matrix is a starting point for unfamiliar multivariate data. Its approximate row target is 10,000–100,000; field count and latency are unspecified. Brushing should probably set two-field filters and dim nonmatching context. Lasso and filters without a chart are desired extensions.

Image export has a new dedicated initiative. Slides and PDF reports remain possible later outputs.

Missing-value rules and derived-output reuse require explanation, not another compulsory product decision. Pairwise-valid cells are an agent recommendation, not a user-approved rule. Additional measure demand is unknown. Full-state saving is a tentative expectation; filter-free templates and derived-result pipelines remain seeds.
