## What needs proof

Retention already has future-cell blanks, partial hatching, tracing and active-entity selection.
Its partial flag rounds the latest timestamp through the end of that UTC date.
Daily cells therefore never become partial. Weekly and monthly cells lose that flag on their final date.
The broad docs explanation does not state these exceptions.
This source/prose discrepancy needs a bounded contract check, not automatic replacement of the rule.

All pools reached cohorts, including partial cells. It has no partial cue or summary trace.
In the proposed fixture, excluding partial cells changes Week 1 from 2/3 to 1/2.
That changes the contributing population. Keep it separate from observation metadata.

Local selection/export/undo/reopen trust remains first.
Then compare the [eight-row fixture](originals/proof-plan.md) with actual behavior and one named reading task.
The new fixture and browser/author checks remain proposed and unrun.
Existing Retention tests passed during preparation; they do not accept these new scenarios.

An endpoint requires authoritative coverage facts and a clear applicable population.
One end cannot express interior gaps or unequal entity coverage.
Upstream preparation is an alternative with authoring and contributor-inspection costs.
If a lesson earns work, pair “Read the observation window” in retention.mdx with its runnable example.
Neither a lesson nor a product change was implemented here.
