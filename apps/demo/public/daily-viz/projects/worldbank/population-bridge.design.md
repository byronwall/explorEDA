# Where the world added 1.9 billion people

**Question.** Which regions account for the world's population growth since 2000?

- **Encoding.** A bridge: the 2000 total, one signed step per region for its change to 2023, and the 2023 total. Step length is people added.
- **Why this form.** Two stacked bars for 2000 and 2023 would make you subtract segments by eye. The bridge does the subtraction and puts every region's change on one scale.

## What this design gives up

Rates. A region that grew fast from a small base looks small here; Sub-Saharan Africa nearly doubled, while East Asia grew by about a sixth.
