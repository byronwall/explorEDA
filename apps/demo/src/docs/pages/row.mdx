## How it reads

- Each bar counts the rows in one category of the chart's field.
- To rank something other than a count, pick a **Measure** and an **Operation** (sum or average, optionally once per ID). This saves a named grouped summary that bar charts can read too, so both show the same numbers.
- Click a bar to filter every other chart to that category.
- Categories keep their all-rows order while other charts filter. The filtered value draws over a faint all-rows bar, or a tick for averages.
- Negative values draw left of a zero line.
- Row height stays between the minimum and maximum you set. When the rows would be shorter than the minimum, the chart shows fewer categories. Set **Top N** to keep the same categories, and the same members in **Other**, at every size.
- Turn on **Use as color field** to give each category its own color from a shared scale, which other charts that color by the same field reuse.

## Annotations

The **Notes** tab adds lines and bands on the horizontal value axis, and callouts that attach to a row by its category, at the end of its bar.
