## Shared color scales

Charts that color by the same field share its scale, so a category has one color across the dashboard. Choosing a field that another chart already colors by joins that chart's scale; the field list shows how many charts use each one.

- **Categorical** scales give each value its own color from a palette, such as Okabe Ito, Tableau 10, or the current theme's palette.
- **Numerical** scales map values onto a continuous color ramp.
- Open **Colors** in the toolbar to change a scale's palette and order. The panel warns when two colors look alike to readers with color vision deficiencies.

## Legends

On a scatter plot, the legend's title is the chart's color field. Click it, or focus it and press Enter, to choose another field or **None**. Alt-click or Alt-Enter still traces the scale.

A <DocsLink topic="color-legend">color legend</DocsLink> shows any shared scale on its own, so one key can explain several charts.
