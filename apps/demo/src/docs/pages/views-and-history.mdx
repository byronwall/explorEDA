## Saved views

The demo keeps several views over the same rows, as tabs above the workspace.

- **New view** opens a blank tab on the same rows. Each view keeps its own charts and filters. Calculations, colors, and field settings stay shared.
- Rename a view in its tab.

## Undo and history

Every change becomes one step in the view's history.

- **Undo** and **Redo** follow the platform keys: <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> and <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd>.
- **History** (<kbd>H</kbd>) shows every saved change on a timeline, newest first. Preview or restore any step.

These are features of the demo site. An app that embeds the package records changes through `onStateChange`; see <DocsLink topic="developers">Developers</DocsLink>.
